# ⏱️ Time, Speed & Distance (TSD)

Time, Speed & Distance is one of the most important aptitude topics for placements and competitive exams. Most questions are based on a few simple formulas, but the challenge is applying them quickly.

Common question types:

* Basic Speed, Distance, Time
* Average Speed
* Relative Speed
* Trains
* Boats & Streams
* Circular Tracks
* Races

---

# 📖 Basic Concepts

There are three fundamental quantities:

* Distance (D)
* Speed (S)
* Time (T)

Relationship:

```text
Distance = Speed × Time
```

From this:

```text
Speed = Distance / Time
```

```text
Time = Distance / Speed
```

---

# 🚗 Units Conversion

### Speed Conversion

```text
1 m/s = 18/5 km/hr
```

```text
1 km/hr = 5/18 m/s
```

---

## Example

Convert 72 km/hr into m/s.

```text
72 × 5/18

= 20 m/s
```

✅ Answer: 20 m/s

---

## Example

Convert 15 m/s into km/hr.

```text
15 × 18/5

= 54 km/hr
```

✅ Answer: 54 km/hr

---

# Basic Problems

## Example 1

A car travels at 60 km/hr for 3 hours.

Find distance.

```text
Distance = Speed × Time

= 60 × 3

= 180 km
```

✅ Answer: 180 km

---

## Example 2

A train covers 240 km in 4 hours.

Find speed.

```text
Speed = 240/4

= 60 km/hr
```

✅ Answer: 60 km/hr

# Average Speed

Many students make mistakes here.

Formula:

```text
Average Speed

= Total Distance / Total Time
```

---

## Example

A car travels:

```text
60 km at 30 km/hr
```

and

```text
60 km at 60 km/hr
```

Time taken:

```text
60/30 = 2 hr
```

```text
60/60 = 1 hr
```

Total:

```text
Distance = 120 km

Time = 3 hr
```

Average Speed:

```text
120/3

= 40 km/hr
```

✅ Answer: 40 km/hr

---

# Shortcut for Equal Distances

When equal distances are covered at speeds a and b:

```text
Average Speed

= (2ab)/(a+b)
```

---

## Example

Speeds:

```text
30 km/hr and 60 km/hr
```

```text
(2×30×60)/(30+60)

= 3600/90

= 40 km/hr
```

✅ Answer: 40 km/hr

---

# Relative Speed

Used when two objects move simultaneously.

---

## Same Direction

Formula:

```text
Relative Speed

= Difference of Speeds
```

Example:

```text
Car A = 60 km/hr
Car B = 40 km/hr
```

```text
Relative Speed

= 60 - 40

= 20 km/hr
```

✅ Answer: 20 km/hr

---

## Opposite Direction

Formula:

```text
Relative Speed

= Sum of Speeds
```

Example:

```text
50 km/hr and 70 km/hr
```

```text
50 + 70

= 120 km/hr
```

✅ Answer: 120 km/hr

---

# Meeting Problems

## Example

Two cars start from opposite ends.

Distance:

```text
240 km
```

Speeds:

```text

```

Relative speed:

```text

= 60 km/hr
```

# 🚂 Train Problems

Train questions are very common in placements.

---

# Pole Crossing

When a train crosses a pole:

Distance covered:

```text
Length of Train
```

Formula:

```text
Time

= Length of Train / Speed
```

---

## Example

Train length:

```text
120 m
```

Speed:

```text
54 km/hr
```

Convert speed:

```text
54 × 5/18

= 15 m/s
```

Time:

```text
120/15

= 8 sec
```

✅ Answer: 8 seconds

---

# Crossing a Platform

Distance covered:

```text
Length of Train + Length of Platform
```

Formula:

```text
Time

= (Train Length + Platform Length)/Speed
```

---

## Example

Train:

```text
150 m
```

Platform:

```text
250 m
```

Speed:

```text
20 m/s
```

Distance:

```text
150 + 250

= 400 m
```

Time:

```text
400/20

= 20 sec
```

✅ Answer: 20 seconds

---

# Two Trains Crossing

---

## Opposite Direction

Formula:

```text
Time

= Sum of Lengths / Sum of Speeds
```

---

### Example

Train A:

```text
120 m
```

Train B:

```text
180 m
```

Speeds:

```text
54 km/hr and 36 km/hr
```

```text

```

Distance:

```text
120 + 180

= 300 m
```

Relative speed:

```text
15 + 10

= 25 m/s
```

Time:

```text
300/25

= 12 sec
```

✅ Answer: 12 sec

---

## Same Direction

Formula:

```text

= Sum of Lengths / Difference of Speeds
```

# 🌊 Boats and Streams

A favorite topic in aptitude exams.

---

# Definitions

### Downstream

Boat moves with current.

```text
Downstream Speed

= Boat Speed + Stream Speed
```

---

### Upstream

Boat moves against current.

```text
Upstream Speed

= Boat Speed - Stream Speed
```

---

# Finding Boat Speed

Formula:

```text
Boat Speed

= (Downstream + Upstream)/2
```

---

## Example

Downstream:

```text
20 km/hr
```

Upstream:

```text
12 km/hr
```

```text
(20 + 12)/2

= 16 km/hr
```

✅ Boat Speed = 16 km/hr

---

# Finding Stream Speed

Formula:

```text
Stream Speed

= (Downstream - Upstream)/2
```

Example:

```text
(20 - 12)/2

= 4 km/hr
```

✅ Stream Speed = 4 km/hr

# 🚀 Important Shortcuts

## If Speed Increases

Time decreases.

Relationship:

```text
Speed ∝ 1/Time
```

---

## Example

Speed increases by:

```text
25%
```

New speed:

```text
125%
```

Time ratio:

```text
100 : 125

= 4 : 5
```

New time decreases by:

```text
20%
```

---

# Speed Change Formula

If speed increases by x%.

Time decrease:

```text
[x/(100+x)] × 100
```

---

## Example

Speed increases:

```text
25%
```

Time decrease:

```text
25/125 × 100

= 20%
```

✅ Answer: 20%

---

# Important Ratios

| Speed Change | Time Change |
| ------------ | ----------- |
| +25%         | -20%        |
| +20%         | -16.67%     |
| +50%         | -33.33%     |
| +100%        | -50%        |

# ⚠️ Common Mistakes

### Mistake 1

Using average of speeds as average speed.

Wrong:

```text
(30+60)/2

= 45
```

Correct:

```text
40 km/hr
```

when distances are equal.

---

### Mistake 2

Not converting units.

Example:

```text
Length → meters
Speed → km/hr
```

Always convert before solving.

---

### Mistake 3

Using sum instead of difference for same-direction trains.

Remember:

```text
Same Direction → Difference
```

```text
Opposite Direction → Sum
```
