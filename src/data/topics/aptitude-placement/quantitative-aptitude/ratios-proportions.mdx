# ⚖️ Ratio & Proportion

Ratio and Proportion are fundamental topics in Quantitative Aptitude. They are
heavily used in:

- Percentages
- Profit & Loss
- Time & Work
- Partnership
- Mixtures & Alligations
- Data Interpretation

Understanding this topic makes many aptitude questions much easier.

---

# 📖 What is a Ratio?

A **ratio** is a comparison of two quantities having the same units.

Formula:

```text
Ratio = First Quantity : Second Quantity
```

Example:

If there are 20 boys and 30 girls in a class:

```text
Ratio of Boys : Girls

= 20 : 30

= 2 : 3
```

✅ Answer: **2 : 3**

---

# Parts of a Ratio

In:

```text
3 : 5
```

- First term = 3 (Antecedent)
- Second term = 5 (Consequent)

---

# Simplifying Ratios

Divide both numbers by their HCF.

Example:

```text
24 : 36
```

HCF = 12

```text
24 ÷ 12 : 36 ÷ 12

= 2 : 3
```

✅ Simplified Ratio = **2 : 3**

---

# Equivalent Ratios

Ratios representing the same relationship.

Example:

```text
2 : 3
```

Multiply by 2:

```text
4 : 6
```

Multiply by 3:

```text
6 : 9
```

All are equivalent ratios.

---

# Ratio of Fractions

Example:

```text
1/2 : 3/4
```

Multiply both by LCM of denominators (4):

```text
(1/2 × 4) : (3/4 × 4)

= 2 : 3
```

✅ Answer: **2 : 3**

---

# Ratio of Decimals

Example:

```text
0.5 : 1.5
```

Multiply both by 10:

```text
5 : 15

= 1 : 3
```

✅ Answer: **1 : 3**

---

# Dividing a Quantity in a Given Ratio

Example:

Divide ₹500 in the ratio:

```text
2 : 3
```

Total parts:

```text
2 + 3 = 5
```

First share:

```text
(2/5) × 500

= ₹200
```

Second share:

```text
(3/5) × 500

= ₹300
```

✅ Answer:

```text
₹200 and ₹300
```

---

# Finding Ratio from Values

Example:

Ages of A and B are:

```text
20 and 30
```

Ratio:

```text
20 : 30

= 2 : 3
```

✅ Answer: **2 : 3**

---

# Continued Ratio

Example:

```text
2 : 3
3 : 4
```

Continued ratio:

```text
2 : 3 : 4
```

---

# Compound Ratio

Multiply corresponding terms.

Example:

```text
2 : 3
4 : 5
```

Compound ratio:

```text
(2×4) : (3×5)

= 8 : 15
```

✅ Answer: **8 : 15**

---

# 📖 What is Proportion?

A proportion states that two ratios are equal.

Formula:

```text
a : b = c : d
```

or

```text
a/b = c/d
```

Example:

```text
2 : 4 = 3 : 6
```

Because:

```text
2/4 = 3/6
```

Both equal:

```text
1/2
```

---

# Fundamental Property of Proportion

If:

```text
a : b = c : d
```

Then:

```text
a × d = b × c
```

Example:

```text
2 : 3 = 4 : 6
```

Check:

```text
2 × 6 = 12

3 × 4 = 12
```

Both equal.

✅ Proportion verified.

---

# Finding Missing Term

Example:

```text
4 : 8 = x : 20
```

Using:

```text
4 × 20 = 8 × x
```

```text
80 = 8x
```

```text
x = 10
```

✅ Answer: **10**

---

# Fourth Proportional

If:

```text
a : b = c : d
```

Then d is called the fourth proportional.

Example:

```text
2 : 5 = 4 : x
```

```text
2x = 20
```

```text
x = 10
```

✅ Fourth proportional = **10**

---

# Third Proportional

If:

```text
a : b = b : c
```

Then c is called third proportional.

Example:

```text
2 : 4 = 4 : x
```

```text
2x = 16
```

```text
x = 8
```

✅ Third proportional = **8**

---

# Mean Proportional

If:

```text
a : x = x : b
```

Then x is mean proportional.

Formula:

```text
x² = ab
```

```text
x = √ab
```

Example:

```text
4 : x = x : 16
```

```text
x² = 64
```

```text
x = 8
```

✅ Answer: **8**

---

# Direct Proportion

When one quantity increases, the other also increases.

Examples:

- More workers → More work done
- More quantity → More cost

Formula:

```text
x₁/x₂ = y₁/y₂
```

---

### Example

5 books cost ₹100.

What is the cost of 10 books?

```text
5/10 = 100/x
```

```text
5x = 1000
```

```text
x = ₹200
```

✅ Answer: ₹200

---

# Inverse Proportion

When one quantity increases, the other decreases.

Examples:

- More workers → Less time
- Higher speed → Less travel time

Formula:

```text
x₁ × y₁ = x₂ × y₂
```

---

### Example

10 workers complete a job in 12 days.

How many days will 15 workers take?

```text
10 × 12 = 15 × x
```

```text
120 = 15x
```

```text
x = 8
```

✅ Answer: 8 days

---

# Partnership Problems

Partnership is based on:

```text
Investment × Time
```

---

### Example

A invests:

```text
₹5000 for 12 months
```

B invests:

```text
₹6000 for 10 months
```

Profit = ₹66,000

Find shares.

Investment-Time:

```text
A = 5000 × 12

= 60000
```

```text
B = 6000 × 10

= 60000
```

Ratio:

```text
1 : 1
```

Profit Share:

```text
33000 : 33000
```

✅ Both get ₹33,000.

---

# Age Problems Using Ratios

Example:

Present age ratio:

```text
A : B = 3 : 5
```

If A is 18 years old.

```text
3 parts = 18
```

```text
1 part = 6
```

B:

```text
5 × 6 = 30
```

✅ B = 30 years

---

# Percentage to Ratio

### Method

Remove % and simplify.

Example:

```text
40%
```

```text
40 : 100
```

```text
2 : 5
```

✅ Ratio = 2 : 5

---

### Common Conversions

| Percentage | Ratio  |
| ---------- | ------ |
| 10%        | 1 : 10 |
| 20%        | 1 : 5  |
| 25%        | 1 : 4  |
| 50%        | 1 : 2  |
| 75%        | 3 : 4  |
| 80%        | 4 : 5  |

---

# Ratio to Percentage

Example:

```text
3 : 5
```

Percentage:

```text
(3/5) × 100
```

```text
60%
```

✅ Answer: 60%

```text
2 : 4
```

```text
Total parts = 6
```

First:

```text
(2/6) × 1200

= ₹400
```

Second:

```text
(4/6) × 1200

= ₹800
```

✅ Answer:

```text
₹400 and ₹800
```

# ⚠️ Common Mistakes

### Mistake 1

Adding ratios directly.

Wrong:

```text
2:3 + 4:5
```

Use compound ratio when required.

---

### Mistake 2

Using direct proportion instead of inverse proportion.

Remember:

```text
Workers ↑
Time ↓
```

Inverse relationship.

---

### Mistake 3

Ignoring simplification.

Always reduce ratios to simplest form.

Example:

```text
12 : 18
```

```text
2 : 3
```

# ✅ Summary

Ratio compares quantities, while proportion compares ratios. Important concepts
include:

- Simplifying ratios
- Compound and continued ratios
- Direct and inverse proportion
- Mean, third, and fourth proportionals
- Partnership problems
- Age-based ratio questions
- Percentage ↔ Ratio conversions

These concepts are widely used in Time & Work, Profit & Loss, and Data
Interpretation.
