# ⏳ Time & Work

Time & Work is one of the most important aptitude topics for placements and
competitive exams. Most questions revolve around:

- Work Efficiency
- Individual Work
- Combined Work
- Pipes & Cisterns
- Wages
- Work and Time Relationships

The key idea is:

> More efficiency → Less time required Less efficiency → More time required

---

# 📖 Basic Concept

If a person completes a work in **n days**:

```text
One day's work = 1/n
```

Example:

A completes a work in 10 days.

```text
One day's work = 1/10
```

Meaning A finishes 1/10th of the work every day.

---

# Fundamental Formula

```text
Work = Efficiency × Time
```

From this:

```text
Time = Work / Efficiency
```

```text
Efficiency = Work / Time
```

---

# Individual Work Problems

## Example 1

A can complete a work in 20 days.

Find work done in 1 day.

```text
1/20
```

✅ Answer: **1/20**

---

## Example 2

A completes 1/15 of work daily.

Find total days.

```text
1 ÷ (1/15)

= 15
```

✅ Answer: **15 days**

---

# Combined Work

When two or more people work together:

```text
Combined Work

= Sum of Individual Works
```

---

## Example

A completes a work in:

```text
10 days
```

B completes the same work in:

```text
15 days
```

One day's work:

```text
A = 1/10
```

```text
B = 1/15
```

Together:

```text
1/10 + 1/15
```

LCM = 30

```text
= 3/30 + 2/30

= 5/30

= 1/6
```

Total time:

```text
6 days
```

✅ Answer: **6 days**

---

# Shortcut Formula for Two Persons

If:

```text
A completes in x days
B completes in y days
```

Together:

```text
(xy)/(x+y)
```

---

## Example

A = 10 days

B = 15 days

```text
(10×15)/(10+15)

= 150/25

= 6
```

✅ Answer: **6 days**

---

# Three Persons Working Together

Example:

```text
A = 10 days
B = 15 days
C = 30 days
```

One day's work:

```text
1/10 + 1/15 + 1/30
```

LCM = 30

```text
3/30 + 2/30 + 1/30

= 6/30

= 1/5
```

Time:

```text
5 days
```

✅ Answer: **5 days**

---

# Efficiency Concept

Efficiency is inversely proportional to time.

Formula:

```text
Efficiency ∝ 1/Time
```

---

## Example

A finishes work in:

```text
10 days
```

B finishes work in:

```text
20 days
```

Efficiency ratio:

```text
20 : 10

= 2 : 1
```

✅ A is twice as efficient as B.

---

# Ratio-Based Questions

Example:

Efficiency ratio:

```text
A : B

= 3 : 5
```

If A takes 20 days.

Time ratio:

```text
5 : 3
```

B's time:

```text
20 × 3/5

= 12 days
```

✅ Answer: **12 days**

---

# LCM Method (Most Important Shortcut)

Instead of assuming work = 1, assume work = LCM of days.

This avoids fractions.

---

## Example

A completes work in:

```text
12 days
```

B completes work in:

```text
18 days
```

Take total work:

```text
LCM(12,18)

= 36 units
```

Efficiency:

```text
A = 36/12 = 3 units/day
```

```text
B = 36/18 = 2 units/day
```

Combined:

```text
5 units/day
```

Time:

```text
36/5

= 7.2 days
```

✅ Answer: **7.2 days**

---

# Work Left Questions

## Example

A completes work in:

```text
20 days
```

Works for:

```text
5 days
```

Completed:

```text
5 × (1/20)

= 1/4
```

Remaining:

```text
1 - 1/4

= 3/4
```

✅ Work left = 3/4

---

# Alternate Day Work

Example:

A completes work in:

```text
12 days
```

B completes work in:

```text
18 days
```

They work on alternate days starting with A.

One 2-day cycle:

```text
1/12 + 1/18
```

LCM = 36

```text
3/36 + 2/36

= 5/36
```

Work after 6 days:

```text
3 cycles

= 15/36
```

Continue until complete.

---

# Wages and Work

Wages are proportional to work done.

Formula:

```text
Wages ∝ Work
```

---

## Example

A and B work together.

Work ratio:

```text
3 : 2
```

Total wage:

```text
₹5000
```

A's share:

```text
(3/5) × 5000

= ₹3000
```

B's share:

```text
(2/5) × 5000

= ₹2000
```

✅ Answer:

```text
₹3000 and ₹2000
```

---

# Men and Work

If:

```text
10 men complete work in 20 days
```

Total work:

```text
10 × 20

= 200 man-days
```

---

## Example

How many days will 20 men take?

```text
200/20

= 10 days
```

✅ Answer: **10 days**

---

# Men, Women, and Children

Example:

```text
1 man = 2 women
```

```text
1 woman = 3 children
```

Then:

```text
1 man = 6 children
```

Convert everything to one unit before solving.

# Pipes and Cisterns

Very common placement topic.

Treat pipes like workers.

---

# Filling Pipe

If pipe fills tank in:

```text
10 hours
```

One hour work:

```text
1/10
```

---

# Emptying Pipe

If pipe empties tank in:

```text
15 hours
```

One hour work:

```text
-1/15
```

Negative because it removes water.

---

# Example

Pipe A fills in:

```text
10 hours
```

Pipe B empties in:

```text
15 hours
```

Net work:

```text
1/10 - 1/15
```

LCM = 30

```text
3/30 - 2/30

= 1/30
```

Time:

```text
30 hours
```

✅ Answer: **30 hours**

---

# ⚠️ Common Mistakes

### Mistake 1

Adding days directly.

Wrong:

```text
10 + 15 = 25
```

Use:

```text
1/10 + 1/15
```

---

### Mistake 2

Forgetting inverse relationship.

```text
Efficiency ↑
Time ↓
```

---

### Mistake 3

Treating emptying pipes as positive work.

Remember:

```text
Filling = Positive
```

```text
Emptying = Negative
```
