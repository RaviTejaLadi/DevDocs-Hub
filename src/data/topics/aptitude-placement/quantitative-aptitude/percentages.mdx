# 📊 Percentages

## Introduction

Percentages are one of the most important topics in Quantitative Aptitude.
Questions from percentages frequently appear in placement tests, banking exams,
government exams, CAT, SSC, and aptitude interviews.

Many topics such as Profit & Loss, Simple Interest, Compound Interest, Data
Interpretation, and Ratio & Proportion are directly based on percentages.

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

After completing this chapter, you will be able to:

- Understand percentage concepts
- Convert fractions to percentages
- Convert percentages to fractions
- Convert decimals to percentages
- Calculate percentage increase and decrease
- Solve aptitude questions quickly using shortcuts

---

# What is Percentage?

The word **Percent** means:

```text id="percent-1"
Per Hundred
```

Symbol:

```text id="percent-2"
%
```

---

## Formula

Percentage can be calculated using:

```text id="percent-3"
Percentage = (Part / Whole) × 100
```

---

## Example

Find percentage of 20 out of 80.

```text id="percent-4"
Percentage = (20 / 80) × 100

= 0.25 × 100

= 25%
```

**Answer:** 25%

# Finding Percentage of a Number

Formula:

```text id="percent-17"
Percentage of Number

= (Percentage / 100) × Number
```

---

## Example

Find:

```text id="percent-18"
20% of 150
```

Solution:

```text id="percent-19"
(20/100) × 150

= 30
```

**Answer:** 30

---

# Finding What Percent One Number is of Another

Formula:

```text id="percent-20"
(Number / Total) × 100
```

---

## Example

What percent of 50 is 15?

```text id="percent-21"
(15 / 50) × 100

= 30%
```

**Answer:** 30%

# Percentage Increase

Formula:

```text id="percent-22"
Increase %

= (Increase / Original Value) × 100
```

---

## Example

Salary increased from ₹20,000 to ₹25,000.

Increase:

```text id="percent-23"
25000 - 20000

= 5000
```

Percentage Increase:

```text id="percent-24"
(5000 / 20000) × 100

= 25%
```

**Answer:** 25%

---

# Percentage Decrease

Formula:

```text id="percent-25"
Decrease %

= (Decrease / Original Value) × 100
```

---

## Example

Price reduced from ₹500 to ₹400.

Decrease:

```text id="percent-26"
500 - 400

= 100
```

Percentage Decrease:

```text id="percent-27"
(100 / 500) × 100

= 20%
```

**Answer:** 20%

---

# Successive Percentage Change

When multiple percentage changes occur one after another:

Formula:

```text id="percent-28"
Net Change

= a + b + (ab/100)
```

Where:

- a = First percentage change
- b = Second percentage change

---

## Example

Increase by:

```text id="percent-29"
20%
```

Then increase by:

```text id="percent-30"
10%
```

Net Change:

```text id="percent-31"
20 + 10 + (20×10)/100

= 30 + 2

= 32%
```

**Answer:** 32%

# Practice Questions

## Easy

1. Find 25% of 200.
2. Convert 0.8 into percentage.
3. Convert 40% into fraction.
4. What percent of 80 is 20?
5. Find 10% of 950.

---

## Medium

1. A salary increases from ₹30,000 to ₹36,000. Find percentage increase.
2. Convert 87.5% into fraction.
3. Find 15% of 640.
4. A price drops from ₹500 to ₹425. Find percentage decrease.
5. What percentage of 240 is 72?

---

## Hard

1. Increase a number by 20% and then decrease it by 10%. Find net percentage
   change.
2. A shopkeeper marks an item 25% above cost price. Find the marked price if
   cost price is ₹800.
3. Population increased from 20,000 to 24,000. Find percentage growth.
4. If 60% of a class are boys and total students are 250, find number of boys.
5. A value decreases by 25% and becomes 150. Find original value.

- Fractions, decimals, and percentages are interchangeable.
- Percentage increase and decrease are frequently asked in exams.
- Many aptitude topics are built on percentage concepts.
- Strong percentage skills significantly improve problem-solving speed.
