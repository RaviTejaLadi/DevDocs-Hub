# Gravitation

## Newton's Law of Gravitation

### Statement
Every particle of matter in the universe attracts every other particle with a force directly proportional to the product of their masses and inversely proportional to the square of the distance between them.

### Equation
```
F = G(m₁m₂)/r²
```

Where:
- G = 6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ N⋅m²/kg²
- r = distance between centers

### Vector Form
```
F₁₂ = -G(m₁m₂)r̂/r²
```

## Gravitational Field

### Definition
```
g = F/m = GM/r²
```

### Field Lines
- Point toward mass
- Strength decreases as 1/r²

### Gravitational Potential
```
V = -GM/r
```

### Gravitational Potential Energy
```
U = -GMm/r
```

(Zero at infinity)

## Acceleration Due to Gravity

### Variation with Altitude
```
g' = g(1 - 2h/R) for h << R
```

### Variation with Depth
```
g' = g(1 - d/R)
```

Where d = depth below surface

### Variation with Latitude
- Centrifugal effect
- Earth's oblateness

### Variation with Rotation
```
g_eff = g - Rω²cos²λ
```

## Kepler's Laws

### First Law (Law of Ellipses)
All planets move in elliptical orbits with the Sun at one focus.

### Second Law (Law of Areas)
A line segment joining a planet and the Sun sweeps out equal areas during equal time intervals.

### Third Law (Law of Periods)
```
T² ∝ a³
```
or
```
T² = (4π²/GM)a³
```

### Derivation from Newton's Law
- For circular orbits: T² = (4π²/GM)r³

## Orbital Mechanics

### Orbital Velocity
```
v_orbital = √(GM/r)
```

### Escape Velocity
```
v_escape = √(2GM/r)
```

### Geosynchronous Orbit
- Period = 24 hours
- Altitude ≈ 35,786 km
- Stationary relative to Earth's surface

### Types of Orbits
1. **Circular**: e = 0
2. **Elliptical**: 0 < e < 1
3. **Parabolic**: e = 1
4. **Hyperbolic**: e > 1

## Gravitational Waves

### Prediction
- Predicted by Einstein's theory of general relativity
- Generated by accelerating masses

### Detection
- LIGO observatory
- First detection in 2015

### Properties
- Travel at speed of light
- Transverse waves
- Quadrupole radiation

### Satellite Motion

- Weather satellites

### Spacecraft Trajectories
- Hohmann transfer orbits
- Gravity assist

### Tidal Forces
- Differential gravitational force
- Tidal bulges
- Effects of Moon and Sun

### Black Holes
- Escaped velocity > c
