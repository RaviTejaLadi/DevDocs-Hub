# Units & Measurements

## Importance of Measurement

Measurement is the foundation of physics, allowing quantitative description and
prediction of natural phenomena. Without accurate measurement, scientific
progress would be impossible.

## International System of Units (SI)

### Base Units

| Quantity    | SI Unit       | Definition                                   |
| ----------- | ------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Length      | meter (m)     | Distance light travels in 1/299,792,458 s    |
| Mass        | kilogram (kg) | Planck constant h = 6.62607015×10⁻³⁴ J⋅s     |
| Time        | second (s)    | 9,192,631,770 oscillations of Cs-133         |
| Current     | ampere (A)    | Elementary charge e = 1.602176634×10⁻¹⁹ C    |
| Temperature | kelvin (K)    | Boltzmann constant k = 1.380649×10⁻²³ J/K    |
| Amount      | mole (mol)    | Avogadro constant Nₐ = 6.02214076×10²³ mol⁻¹ |
| Luminosity  | candela (cd)  | Luminous efficacy of 540 THz radiation       |

### Derived Units

| Quantity  | Unit   | Symbol | Base Units |
| --------- | ------ | ------ | ---------- |
| Force     | newton | N      | kg⋅m⋅s⁻²   |
| Energy    | joule  | J      | kg⋅m²⋅s⁻²  |
| Power     | watt   | W      | kg⋅m²⋅s⁻³  |
| Pressure  | pascal | Pa     | kg⋅m⁻¹⋅s⁻² |
| Frequency | hertz  | Hz     | s⁻¹        |

## Measurement Instruments

- **Length**: Ruler, caliper, micrometer, laser interferometer
- **Mass**: Balance, electronic scale
- **Time**: Stopwatch, atomic clock
- **Temperature**: Thermometer, thermocouple
- **Current**: Ammeter, galvanometer

### Uses of Dimensional Analysis

2. Deriving relationships between quantities
3. Converting between unit systems

## Error Analysis and Uncertainties

### Types of Errors

- **Systematic errors**: Consistent biases in measurement
- **Random errors**: Fluctuations in measurement
- **Gross errors**: Mistakes in procedure or recording

### Uncertainty Propagation

- **Addition/Subtraction**: δz = √[(δx)² + (δy)²]
- **Multiplication/Division**: δz/z = √[(δx/x)² + (δy/y)²]

## Unit Conversion

### Examples

- 1 m = 100 cm = 1000 mm = 3.28 ft
- 1 kg = 1000 g = 2.204 lb
- 1 J = 1 N⋅m = 0.239 cal
- 1 atm = 101.325 kPa = 760 mmHg
