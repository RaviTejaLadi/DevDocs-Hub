# 🚗 Time, Speed and Distance

## Introduction

Time, Speed and Distance (TSD) is one of the most important topics in Quantitative Aptitude. Questions from this topic are frequently asked in placement tests, SSC, Banking, Railways, CAT, UPSC, and other competitive exams.

Most problems are based on the relationship between distance traveled, speed of movement, and time taken.

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

After completing this chapter, you will be able to:

* Understand the relationship between Time, Speed, and Distance
* Convert speed units correctly
* Solve train problems
* Solve average speed problems
* Solve relative speed problems
* Solve boats and streams questions

---

# Basic Formula

The entire chapter revolves around one formula:

Distance = Speed \times Time

From this:

### Speed

```text id="tsd-1"
Speed = Distance / Time
```

### Time

```text id="tsd-2"
Time = Distance / Speed
```

---

# Units of Measurement

| Quantity | Unit                    |

| Distance | km, m                   |
| Speed    | km/h, m/s               |

# Speed Conversion

## km/h to m/s

Formula:

```text id="tsd-3"
Multiply by 5/18
```

### Example

```text id="tsd-4"
72 km/h
```

```text id="tsd-5"
72 × 5/18

= 20 m/s
```

---

## m/s to km/h

Formula:

```text id="tsd-6"
Multiply by 18/5
```

### Example

```text id="tsd-7"
25 m/s
```

```text id="tsd-8"
25 × 18/5

= 90 km/h
```

---

# Finding Distance

Formula:

```text id="tsd-9"
Distance = Speed × Time
```

---

## Example

A car travels at:

```text id="tsd-10"
60 km/h
```

for

```text id="tsd-11"
4 hours
```

Distance:

```text id="tsd-12"
60 × 4

= 240 km
```

**Answer:** 240 km

---

# Finding Speed

Formula:

```text id="tsd-13"
Speed = Distance / Time
```

---

## Example

A train covers:

```text id="tsd-14"
300 km
```

in

```text id="tsd-15"
5 hours
```

Speed:

```text id="tsd-16"
300 ÷ 5

= 60 km/h
```

**Answer:** 60 km/h

---

# Finding Time

Formula:

```text id="tsd-17"
Time = Distance / Speed
```

---

## Example

Distance:

```text id="tsd-18"
240 km
```

Speed:

```text id="tsd-19"
80 km/h
```

Time:

```text id="tsd-20"
240 ÷ 80

= 3 hours
```

**Answer:** 3 hours

---

# Average Speed

Average speed is not simply the average of two speeds unless equal distances are covered.

Formula:

Average\ Speed = \frac{Total\ Distance}{Total\ Time}

---

## Example

A person travels:

* 60 km at 30 km/h
* 60 km at 60 km/h

Time taken:

```text id="tsd-21"
60/30 = 2 hours

60/60 = 1 hour
```

Total:

```text id="tsd-22"
Distance = 120 km

Time = 3 hours
```

Average Speed:

```text id="tsd-23"
120 ÷ 3

= 40 km/h
```

**Answer:** 40 km/h

---

# Shortcut for Equal Distances

If equal distances are covered at speeds:

```text id="tsd-24"
a and b
```

Average Speed:

```text id="tsd-25"
2ab / (a + b)
```

---

## Example

Speeds:

```text id="tsd-26"
40 km/h and 60 km/h
```

Average Speed:

```text id="tsd-27"
2 × 40 × 60

----------------
40 + 60

= 48 km/h
```

**Answer:** 48 km/h

---

# Relative Speed

Relative speed depends on direction of movement.

---

## Moving in Same Direction

Formula:

```text id="tsd-28"
Relative Speed

= Difference of Speeds
```

### Example

```text id="tsd-29"
80 km/h and 50 km/h
```

Relative speed:

```text id="tsd-30"
80 - 50

= 30 km/h
```

---

## Moving in Opposite Directions

Formula:

```text id="tsd-31"
Relative Speed

= Sum of Speeds
```

### Example

```text id="tsd-32"
40 km/h and 60 km/h
```

```text id="tsd-33"
40 + 60

= 100 km/h
```

---

# Train Problems

Train questions are based on:

```text id="tsd-34"
Distance = Length of Train
```

or

```text id="tsd-35"
Distance = Sum of Lengths
```

---

## Crossing a Pole

Formula:

```text id="tsd-36"
Time

= Length of Train / Speed
```

---

### Example

Train length:

```text id="tsd-37"
120 m
```

Speed:

```text id="tsd-38"
20 m/s
```

Time:

```text id="tsd-39"
120 ÷ 20

= 6 seconds
```

**Answer:** 6 seconds

---

# Crossing a Platform

Formula:

```text id="tsd-40"
Time

= (Train Length + Platform Length)

÷ Speed
```

---

## Example

Train:

```text id="tsd-41"
150 m
```

Platform:

```text id="tsd-42"
100 m
```

Speed:

```text id="tsd-43"
25 m/s
```

Time:

```text id="tsd-44"
(150 + 100)

÷ 25

= 10 seconds
```

**Answer:** 10 seconds

---

# Boats and Streams

---

## Downstream Speed

Formula:

```text id="tsd-45"
Boat Speed + Stream Speed
```

---

## Upstream Speed

Formula:

```text id="tsd-46"
Boat Speed - Stream Speed
```

---

## Example

Boat speed:

```text id="tsd-47"
12 km/h
```

Stream speed:

```text id="tsd-48"
4 km/h
```

Downstream:

```text id="tsd-49"
12 + 4

= 16 km/h
```

Upstream:

```text id="tsd-50"
12 - 4

= 8 km/h
```

# Solved Examples

## Example 1

Find distance traveled by a bus moving at 70 km/h for 5 hours.

Solution:

```text id="tsd-51"
Distance

= 70 × 5

= 350 km
```

**Answer:** 350 km

---

## Example 2

A car covers 180 km in 3 hours.

Find speed.

Solution:

```text id="tsd-52"
Speed

= 180 ÷ 3

= 60 km/h
```

**Answer:** 60 km/h

---

## Example 3

Convert:

```text id="tsd-53"
54 km/h
```

into m/s.

Solution:

```text id="tsd-54"
54 × 5/18

= 15 m/s
```

**Answer:** 15 m/s

---

## Example 4

Two trains move towards each other at:

```text id="tsd-55"
40 km/h and 60 km/h
```

Relative speed:

```text id="tsd-56"
40 + 60

= 100 km/h
```

**Answer:** 100 km/h

---

## Example 5

A train of length 200 m crosses a pole in 20 seconds.

Find speed.

Solution:

```text id="tsd-57"
Speed

= 200 ÷ 20

= 10 m/s
```

**Answer:** 10 m/s

# Practice Questions

## Easy

1. Find distance covered at 50 km/h in 4 hours.
2. Convert 90 km/h into m/s.
3. Find speed if 240 km is covered in 4 hours.
4. Find time required to travel 180 km at 60 km/h.
5. Convert 15 m/s into km/h.

---

## Medium

1. A train 150 m long crosses a pole in 15 seconds. Find speed.
2. Find average speed for equal distances at 40 km/h and 80 km/h.
3. Two cars travel in opposite directions at 45 km/h and 55 km/h. Find relative speed.
4. A boat moves at 20 km/h in still water and stream speed is 5 km/h. Find downstream speed.
5. A train crosses a 200 m platform in 20 seconds at 25 m/s. Find train length.

---

## Hard

1. A train 180 m long crosses another train 120 m long moving in opposite directions in 10 seconds. Find relative speed.
2. A boat travels 48 km upstream in 6 hours and the same distance downstream in 4 hours. Find boat speed and stream speed.
3. A car travels half the distance at 40 km/h and the remaining half at 60 km/h. Find average speed.
4. Two trains moving in the same direction at 72 km/h and 54 km/h cross each other in 30 seconds. Find relative speed.
5. A train passes a platform and pole in 25 seconds and 15 seconds respectively. Find the ratio of platform length to train length.

# Quick Tricks

✅ Remember the TSD triangle:

```text id="tsd-58"
      Distance
     /        \
 Speed      Time
```

Cover the required variable to get the formula.

---

✅ Opposite directions → Add speeds.

✅ Same direction → Subtract speeds.

✅ Equal distances → Use harmonic mean shortcut.

✅ Train crossing pole → Only train length matters.

✅ Boats and Streams:

```text id="tsd-59"
Downstream = Boat + Stream

Upstream = Boat - Stream
```

---

# Common Exam Mistakes

❌ Forgetting unit conversion.

❌ Using average of speeds directly.

❌ Adding speeds when objects move in the same direction.

❌ Ignoring platform length in train problems.

---

# Key Takeaways

* Distance = Speed × Time.
* Speed and time are inversely proportional.
* Relative speed depends on direction.
* Average speed must use total distance and total time.
* Train problems rely on distance covered by train length.
* Boats and streams use addition and subtraction of speeds.
* TSD is one of the highest-scoring aptitude topics when formulas are mastered.
