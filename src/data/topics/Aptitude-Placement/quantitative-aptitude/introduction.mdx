# 📖 Introduction to Quantitative Aptitude

## What is Quantitative Aptitude?

Quantitative Aptitude is the branch of mathematics that focuses on solving numerical problems quickly and accurately. It tests a person's ability to understand numbers, perform calculations, analyze data, and apply mathematical concepts to real-world situations.

It is one of the most important sections in competitive exams, campus placements, government recruitment tests, banking exams, aptitude tests, and technical interviews.

---

## 🎯 Why Learn Quantitative Aptitude?

Quantitative Aptitude helps improve:

* Logical thinking
* Problem-solving skills
* Numerical ability
* Decision-making skills
* Calculation speed
* Analytical reasoning

These skills are useful not only in exams but also in daily life and professional careers.

## 🧠 Basic Mathematical Operations

The foundation of Quantitative Aptitude relies on four basic operations:

### Addition (+)

Combining two or more numbers.

**Example**

```text
25 + 15 = 40
```

### Subtraction (-)

Finding the difference between numbers.

**Example**

```text
50 - 20 = 30
```

### Multiplication (×)

Repeated addition of a number.

**Example**

```text
12 × 5 = 60
```

### Division (÷)

Splitting a number into equal parts.

**Example**

```text
100 ÷ 4 = 25
```

## ⚡ Common Calculation Tricks

### Multiplying by 5

Multiply by 10 and divide by 2.

**Example**

```text
48 × 5

= 480 ÷ 2
= 240
```

---

### Finding 10%

Move decimal one place left.

**Example**

```text
10% of 850

= 85
```

---

### Finding 50%

Simply divide by 2.

**Example**

```text
50% of 360

= 180
```

## 📝 Sample Aptitude Questions

### Question 1

What is 25% of 200?

**Solution**

```text
25/100 × 200

= 50
```

**Answer:** 50

---

### Question 2

A shopkeeper buys an item for ₹400 and sells it for ₹500. Find the profit.

**Solution**

```text
Profit = Selling Price − Cost Price

= 500 − 400
= ₹100
```

**Answer:** ₹100

---

### Question 3

A car travels 120 km in 2 hours. Find its speed.

**Solution**

```text
Speed = Distance / Time

= 120 / 2
= 60 km/h
```

**Answer:** 60 km/h
